# **Course Project: Phase 1 — Product Vision & State of the Art Proposal**

**Course:** Software Engineering / Architectural Patterns  
**Deliverable:** Project Proposal Document (PDF / Markdown)  
**Submission Type:** Team Deliverable  
**Weight:** 15% of Final Grade

## **1\. Overview & Objective**

Throughout this semester, your team will design, architect, and build a full-stack software application from the ground up. Before writing code, you must rigorously validate the problem you are solving, define your target users, and analyze existing solutions.  
The goal of **Phase 1** is to establish a clear, well-researched foundation for your project. You will define the core product vision, articulate the problem statement, conduct a competitive analysis, and explore the current state of the art to ensure your solution offers meaningful innovation.

## **2\. Document Structure Requirements**

Your submission must be a structured document organized into the following four sections:

### **Section 1: Executive Summary & Product Vision**

> * **Project Name & Tagline:** A concise, descriptive title and a 1-sentence value proposition.  
> * **Core Purpose:** High-level summary of what the application does, who it is for, and the ultimate outcome it enables.  
> * **Target Audience:** Primary and secondary user personas, including their key goals, pain points, and technical background.

### **Section 2: Problem Statement & Motivation**

> * **The Problem:** Clear, detailed explanation of the specific real-world pain point or inefficiency your application addresses.  
> * **Evidence & Context:** Why does this problem matter now? Provide context, examples, or qualitative/quantitative evidence demonstrating why existing approaches fall short.  
> * **Proposed Solution High-Level Overview:** How your application directly resolves the identified problem.

### **Section 3: Competitive Analysis**

Identify **3 to 4 existing tools, platforms, or competitors** (direct or indirect) that attempt to solve a similar problem or serve a similar target audience.

> * **Comparison Matrix:** Provide a table comparing features, user experience, pricing model (if applicable), strengths, and weaknesses across all identified competitors.  
> * **Gaps in Current Solutions:** Synthesize what existing competitors are missing or failing to execute well.

### **Section 4: State of the Art & Innovation Strategy**

> * **Technical & Domain Research:** Research recent advancements, methodologies, open-source frameworks, or architectural approaches relevant to your domain (e.g., real-time collaboration, AI integration, low-latency sync, offline-first design).  
> * **Differentiators & Innovation:** Clearly state how your project improves upon existing state-of-the-art solutions. What is your unique value proposition (UVP)?  
> * **Initial Feasibility Assessment:** Technical constraints, potential technical risks, and scope boundaries for the semester schedule.

## **3\. Evaluation Criteria**

| Component | Description | Weight   |
